About the job
Overview

The Windows + Devices Fundamentals team is responsible for the reliability, compatibility, performance, battery life, and sustainability of Windows devices. Our work spans hardware and OEM partners, the operating system, and application layers – helping protect, manage, and enhance the fundamentals of the Windows ecosystem.

We are seeking a motivated Software Engineer to help build the next generation of Windows device optimizations. As advances in AI, silicon, and software create new opportunities, you will contribute to making exceptional device fundamentals a defining feature of Windows PCs.

This role is ideal for an early-career engineer who enjoys learning how complex systems work, developing solid technical skills, and contributing to innovations that improve how people live, work, and play.

Microsoft’s mission is to empower every person and every organization on the planet to achieve more. As employees we come together with a growth mindset, innovate to empower others, and collaborate to realize our shared goals. Each day we build on our values of respect, integrity, and accountability to create a culture of inclusion where everyone can thrive at work and beyond.

Responsibilities

Analyze telemetry and trace data, with support from experienced engineers, to understand software and hardware behavior on individual devices and at scale.
Investigate system issues using debugging and performance-analysis tools.
Identify optimization opportunities and contribute to the design and implementation of improvements.
Develop, test, and maintain code that improves experiences across operating-system and application layers.
Deliver reliable, high-quality solutions by following sound engineering practices and incorporating feedback from code and design reviews.
Use data to evaluate solutions, validate improvements, and support engineering decisions.
Collaborate with team members and partner teams to deliver improvements across the Windows ecosystem.

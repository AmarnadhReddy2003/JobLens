About The Department

Legal and Compliance Technology delivers and supports the critical applications that help legal and compliance teams operate efficiently, manage risk, and meet regulatory obligations. 
The team works closely with business stakeholders, technology professionals, and compliance specialists to maintain and enhance solutions supporting trade compliance, 
Know Your Customer/Know Your Distributor (KYC/KYD), agreement management, legal document management, contract lifecycle management, and global regulatory compliance. 
Team members gain exposure to business-critical processes, regulatory technologies, and cross-functional collaboration in a highly impactful environment.
